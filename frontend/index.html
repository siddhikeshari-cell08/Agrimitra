THE PROBLEM

Farm decisions shouldn't have to rely on guesswork.

Limited access to timely soil information can make irrigation and fertilizer decisions harder for small and marginal farmers. Agrimitra focuses on making soil information more accessible and easier to act on.

WHY AGRIMITRA

From raw sensor readings to useful decisions.

A simple farmer-first interface for monitoring, alerts and soil records.

📊

Soil Health Score

Multiple readings are converted into an easy 0–100 score.

💧

Real-Time Monitoring

Monitor moisture and environmental readings continuously.

🤖

AI-Based Insights

Process sensor data to identify abnormal conditions.

🚨

Early Warnings

Highlight readings that require farmer attention.

📄

Farmer Soil Report

Maintain a simple history of soil conditions.

💰

Soil Credit Points

Maintain soil-data records for future financial-service use cases.

HOW IT WORKS

Four simple layers.

01

Sense

Soil moisture, gas, temperature and humidity are collected.

02

Process

ESP8266/NodeMCU collects and processes readings.

03

Score

The analysis layer generates a Soil Health Score from 0–100.

04

Act

Farmers receive insights, alerts and reports.
